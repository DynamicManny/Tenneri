SENTINEL: SOC Training Simulation

The situation

Cybersecurity programmes need analysts to experience a real SOC before they encounter one professionally. The default solution is an expensive third-party platform with per-seat costs, a vendor contract, and an IT security review every renewal cycle.

What changed

SENTINEL runs entirely inside the institution's existing LMS. The SOC is not a link to somewhere else. It is a native content page. The institution owns it outright. The vendor relationship does not exist. The per-seat cost does not apply.

Inside the environment
  • Live alert queue with triage workflow, severity classification, and escalation logic
  • Real-time network traffic chart with animated data and sliding UTC timestamps
  • Threat category breakdown with detection coverage indicators across six categories
  • Analyst workload management with case redistribution and narrative-driven team events
  • Suspicious connection table, IOC confidence scoring, and critical asset status panel
  • Every data layer interconnected: resolved cases, open alerts, and analyst load move together
The outcome

One build. No subscription. No renewal. No dependency. The environment is version-controlled and fully customisable without touching a vendor.

Business Education · Simulation · Native LMS

VENTURE: Supply Chain Decision Lab

The situation

Business students spend years reading case studies about disruptions that happened to someone else's supply chain. The knowledge is there. The instinct is not. Theoretical understanding does not transfer to a decision made under pressure with real consequences attached.

What changed

VENTURE puts students inside the disruption. A supplier goes dark. Every decision they make changes the cost tracker, the delivery window, and the satisfaction score in real time. The cascade is visible before the student fully understands why it is happening. That is the point.

Inside the environment
  • Multi-stage disruption scenario with branching decision paths that compound forward
  • Live cost, timeline, and customer satisfaction indicators responding to every choice
  • Alternative supplier negotiation simulation with visible risk and cost trade-offs
  • Post-scenario debrief with annotated decision review and industry benchmark comparison
  • Instructor dashboard showing each student's decision path and outcome
The outcome

Students leave knowing what a wrong sourcing decision feels like before they make one with a real client's money. That is not something a case study teaches.

Marketing Education · Simulation · Native LMS

CAMPAIGN: Marketing Decision Lab

The situation

Marketing programmes teach strategy through plans that never run and campaigns that never fail. Students learn the language of performance marketing without ever seeing an audience not respond. The gap between the plan and the result stays theoretical until it is someone else's budget on the line.

What changed

CAMPAIGN gives students a brief, a budget, and a market that responds. Allocate too heavily in one channel and watch diminishing returns set in. Miss the demographic and watch the conversion rate flatline. The feedback is immediate and the debrief is built in.

Inside the environment
  • Client brief with defined product, audience profile, and campaign objective
  • Budget allocation across social, search, display, and influencer channels
  • Dynamic audience response engine reacting to targeting and spend decisions
  • Live performance dashboard showing reach, engagement, and conversion building week by week
  • Post-campaign analysis with optimised scenario comparison
  • Instructor view showing each student's allocation strategy and outcome
The outcome

Students arrive at their first real campaign knowing what failure looks like and why it happens. That is the education. The grade is secondary.

Psych 420 · Research Methods & Ethics · Native LMS

IRB: Ethics Review Lab

The situation

Research ethics is typically taught through readings and case studies about violations that happened decades ago. Students understand the principles in the abstract. They do not understand where the lines are until they submit a real proposal and discover they crossed one. By then the learning has a cost attached.

What changed

The IRB Ethics Review Lab puts students inside the review process before they submit anything real. They work through research scenarios, identify ethical concerns, make recommendations, and see the consequences of each call. They find the lines by testing them where the stakes are educational, not professional.

Inside the environment
  • Research scenario library spanning informed consent, participant risk, data privacy, and deception
  • Review workflow mirroring a real IRB process: flag, assess, recommend, approve or reject
  • Consequence mapping showing how each decision affects the study's ethics standing
  • Reflection prompts connecting each scenario to the Belmont Report and APA guidelines
  • Scored completion syncing directly to the Psych 420 gradebook
The outcome

Students submit their first real research proposals having already made the mistakes. In the lab, not in the field. That distinction is the education.

Corporate Training · GRC · Native LMS

THRESHOLD: Compliance Training Lab

The situation

Most employees have completed mandatory compliance training multiple times and remember almost none of it. When training presents rules without consequences, people process it the same way they process terms and conditions. They click through and move on.

What changed

THRESHOLD does not ask employees to memorise rules. It asks them to understand why the rules exist. Every scenario presents a realistic workplace situation where the wrong choice produces a visible, detailed consequence cascade. The cost of a bad decision becomes real before it is real.

Inside the environment
  • Scenario-based GRC situations drawn from data breach, reporting, and escalation contexts
  • Consequence timeline that builds itself after each decision, showing regulatory and financial impact
  • Decision mapping across GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 frameworks where relevant
  • Reading timer on consequence panels ensuring employees cannot skip the outcome
  • Completion record syncing automatically to HR and compliance tracking systems
The outcome

Employees do not finish THRESHOLD because they have to. They finish it because they want to know what happens next. That is the first time most of them have felt that way about compliance training.
