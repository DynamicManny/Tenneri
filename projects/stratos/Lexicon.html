How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, the tiles show how close you were. When the round ends, you get the word's meaning.

Example

Say the word is PRISM and you guess PROSE:

PROSE
  • P and R are green: right letter, right spot.
  • S is gold: it's in the word, but in a different spot.
  • O and E are gray: they're not in the word.
PRISM

All five green and you've solved it.

Schedule

A new word every school day. There's no new word on weekends or holidays, and skipping them won't break your streak. Missed one? Open past puzzles from the calendar button.

Statistics

Practice words don't count. Your streak counts words solved on the day they came out. Weekends and holidays don't break it.

Settings

Hard modeLetters you've found must be used in every later guess. Can only be changed before your first guess.
Dark mode
High contrast colorsBlue and orange instead of green and gold. Easier to tell apart for many people with color blindness.
Voices come from your browser. Edge, Chrome and Safari usually sound the most natural.

Past puzzles

Pick a school day to play its word. Past puzzles count toward games played, but not your streak.
