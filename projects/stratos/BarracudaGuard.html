Warning Signs to Watch For
Spoofed Sender
The address looks like a real company but the domain is slightly off (paypa1.com, not paypal.com)
Suspicious Link
A link that leads somewhere unexpected or asks you to log in
Pressure to Act Fast
Language that creates urgency: "your account will be closed," "respond within 24 hours"
Unexpected Attachment
A file you weren't expecting, especially .exe, .zip, or .js formats
Too Good to Be True
You've won something you didn't enter, or someone's offering an unexpected reward
Impersonation
Someone posing as a colleague, your bank, or a service you use
