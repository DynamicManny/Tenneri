Module 2 · Lesson

Dialing in espresso

6 minIncludes practice

Espresso is a recipe with three numbers: what goes in, what comes out, and how long it takes. Most cafés start here:

18 gcoffee in
36 gespresso out
25 to 30 sto pull the shot

When the shot runs too fast or too slow, you don't change the recipe. You change the grind.

Try it: dial in the grinder

14 sSour and thin

The water rushed through too fast. Grind finer to slow it down.
