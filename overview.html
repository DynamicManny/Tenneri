What we build

Not a new platform.
A new experience inside yours.

Every capability below runs natively inside the LMS or training platform your organization already operates. Nothing external. Nothing that requires a new security review, a new vendor contract, or a new line on next year's budget. Your team retains full administrative control and ownership of every file delivered.

Multi-Path Modules
Readers, watchers, and doers all have a way in. One module, multiple entry points, one gradebook or LMS record. No learner left on one track.
Scenario Labs
Decision-based simulations that put learners inside real situations before they are assessed. Consequences are visible. The logic sticks.
Game-Based Review
Reinforcement activities learners return to voluntarily. Completion rates on optional game-based activities consistently exceed standard module equivalents.
Adaptive Assessments
Unique scenarios per learner, automatic progress saving, and direct LMS gradebook integration. No two learners see the same assessment.
Custom LMS Applications
Zero-dependency builds. Your internal team retains full administrative control and ownership of the source code within your LMS. No ongoing vendor relationship, no renewal risk.
Simulated Environments
Fully functional OS environments, terminals, and application labs running inside the browser. No virtual machines, no per-seat licensing, no infrastructure.

Results in the wild

Three different problems.
One consistent outcome.

Across higher education and corporate training, the challenge is always the same: content exists but does not land. The following engagements show what changes when the design of the experience is taken as seriously as the content itself.

Higher Education
Data Science
When the redesign outlasted the vendor

A program with strong content but the wrong container. Completion sat at 44%. After a full multi-path redesign, readers had depth, watchers had short-form summaries, and doers had scenario labs that put them inside the material before they were assessed. Scores improved 38% and completion nearly doubled.

The institution eliminated its external platform dependency. Licensing costs dropped 68%. Everything rebuilt in-house, owned outright. The external contract was not renewed.

"I retook this course after failing the first version. I cannot believe how different it felt. I actually understood what I was doing this time." A learner · Data Science Program · second attempt after redesign

Higher Education
Emerging Technology
Making the unintuitive feel inevitable

A quantum computing course built in close collaboration with subject matter experts. Each concept scaffolded against the previous one. Activities designed to create moments of recognition rather than confusion. Difficult ideas introduced through analogy, reinforced through application, tested through scenario.

No subject is too complex to teach online. The constraint is almost never the material. It is always the path through it. Learners finished asking when the next course would be available.

"I came in thinking this would be the one course I would just have to survive. I left wanting to go deeper." A learner · Emerging Technology Program · post-course survey

Corporate
Mandatory Compliance
The room nobody wants to be in

Mandatory compliance training where employees were completing modules on autopilot. The redesign led with the personal why: what happens to this specific role in this specific scenario if this goes wrong. Scenario-based modules put employees inside real situations before asking them to apply the rules.

Completion moved from under 60% to above 90%. Post-training assessments showed a measurable improvement in applied knowledge retention, not surface familiarity with the material.

"I actually understood why we have these policies. I had been clicking through that training for three years and this is the first time it made sense." An employee · Systems Training Coordinator · Compliance Redesign
