Cybersecurity Training · Simulation · Native LMS

SENTINEL: SOC Training Simulation

The Problem

Cybersecurity training programs need analysts to experience realistic SOC environments before they encounter one on the job. Traditional approaches depend on expensive simulation platforms, dedicated lab infrastructure, or external tools that add per-seat costs to every cohort.

The Approach

SENTINEL is a full SOC dashboard built entirely in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and deployed as a native LMS content page. No external platforms, no integration work, no additional licensing. It runs on the system the institution already operates.

What it includes
  • Live alert queue with triage workflow, severity classification, and escalation logic
  • Real-time network traffic chart with sliding UTC timestamps and animated data simulation
  • Threat category breakdown with animated detection coverage indicators across six categories
  • Analyst workload management with case redistribution and narrative-driven team events
  • Suspicious connection table, IOC confidence scoring, and critical asset status panel
  • All data layers interconnected: resolved cases, open alerts, and analyst load move together
The result

A single build replaces a per-seat simulation platform subscription for the full duration of the program. The environment is owned outright by the institution, version-controlled, and fully customisable without any vendor relationship.

VENTURE: Supply Chain Decision Lab

📦
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Business students spend their programs reading about supply chains that belong to other companies. When a disruption actually happens, the theoretical knowledge does not transfer because they have never had to make a consequential decision under pressure.

The Approach

VENTURE drops students into a live disruption. A supplier goes dark. Costs are climbing. Delivery windows are closing. Every decision they make changes the landscape for the next one. The consequence chain is visible and immediate.

What it includes
  • Multi-stage disruption scenario with branching decision paths
  • Live cost, timeline, and customer satisfaction indicators that respond to every choice
  • Alternative supplier negotiation simulation with risk and cost trade-offs
  • Post-scenario debrief with annotated decision review and industry benchmark comparison
  • Instructor dashboard showing each student's decision path and outcome
Who it is for

Business Administration, Logistics, and Operations Management programs where case studies are not enough and a live consequence environment would change what students retain.

Marketing Education · Simulation · Native LMS

CAMPAIGN: Marketing Decision Lab

📣
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Marketing courses teach strategy in the abstract. Students write campaign plans that never run and never fail. Without seeing a real audience respond to real choices, the relationship between targeting decisions and outcomes stays theoretical.

The Approach

Students receive a brief, a budget, and three channels. They define the audience, allocate spend, and launch. A simulated market responds dynamically. Over-invest in one channel and watch diminishing returns set in. Miss the demographic and watch the conversion rate drop.

What it includes
  • Client brief with defined product, audience profile, and campaign objective
  • Budget allocation tool across social, search, and display channels
  • Dynamic audience response engine that reacts to targeting and spend decisions
  • Live performance dashboard showing reach, engagement, and conversion in real time
  • Post-campaign analysis with optimised scenario comparison
  • Instructor view showing each student's allocation strategy and result
Who it is for

Marketing, Communications, and Business programs where students need to see their decisions fail and succeed before they do it with a real client's money.

INTAKE: Social Dynamics Lab

🧠
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Psychology programs are heavy on theory and light on application until field experience arrives at the end. Students learn about conformity, persuasion, and group dynamics in lecture, but have no structured opportunity to recognise those mechanisms in motion before clinical placement.

The Approach

INTAKE presents students with real social scenarios drawn from workplace, academic, and community contexts. The goal is not diagnosis. It is recognition. Students identify the psychological mechanism at play, consider the variables driving it, and reflect on what a well-informed response would look like.

What it includes
  • Scenario library spanning conformity, cognitive bias, persuasion, group dynamics, and attribution
  • Multi-choice identification exercises with explanatory feedback grounded in theory
  • Context variation: each scenario has workplace, social, and academic versions
  • Reflection prompts that connect observable behaviour to course concepts
  • Scored completion that syncs to the LMS gradebook
Who it is for

Undergraduate Psychology programs where the gap between theory and field experience is where students struggle most. This fills that gap without overreaching into clinical territory.

Science Education · Virtual Lab · Native LMS

ELEMENT: Virtual Science Lab

⚗️
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Wet labs and physical science equipment are expensive, time-limited, and inaccessible outside of scheduled class hours. Students arrive at the real lab having only read about the experiment, which means the first run is always spent following steps rather than building understanding.

The Approach

ELEMENT lets students run the experiment before they run the experiment. Adjust variables, observe outcomes, make predictions, and arrive at the real lab with the conceptual groundwork already laid. Physics and chemistry modules are built around the relationships between variables, not just the procedures.

What it includes
  • Physics modules: projectile motion, force and acceleration, pendulum dynamics, optics
  • Chemistry modules: titration, reaction rates, gas laws, and acid-base equilibrium
  • Variable adjustment controls with real-time visual output
  • Prediction prompts before each run, reinforcing the hypothesis-test cycle
  • Data recording and graphing built into the lab environment
  • No installation, no external platform, runs inside the LMS content page
Who it is for

K-12 and undergraduate science programs where pre-lab preparation is weak and in-person lab time is too valuable to spend on orientation.

K-12 Education · Interactive Tool · Native LMS

NUMERICS: Math Manipulatives Lab

📐
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Fractions, probability, and geometric reasoning are consistently among the most misunderstood concepts in K-12 mathematics. They are almost always taught through equations and diagrams on a page. The abstract representation does not match how the underlying relationships actually work.

The Approach

NUMERICS makes the abstract visible. Students run probability simulations and watch distributions form across hundreds of trials. They manipulate fraction bars and see equivalence without being told it. They build geometric proofs by dragging and connecting rather than writing. The concept arrives through doing rather than reading.

What it includes
  • Probability simulator: coin flips, dice rolls, and custom events with real-time distribution graphs
  • Fraction manipulatives: visual bars, equivalent fraction matching, and operation demonstrations
  • Geometry tools: area, perimeter, angle relationships, and proof construction
  • Each tool paired with a guided discovery activity and a scored reflection check
  • Teacher view showing class-wide engagement and common error patterns
Who it is for

Middle and high school mathematics teachers who need tools that work inside their existing LMS without adding another login, another subscription, or another platform to manage.

K-12 Education · Literacy Tool · Native LMS

SIGNAL: Reading Comprehension Lab

📖
Demo coming soon
The Problem

Most reading comprehension tools test recall. Students find the sentence that contains the answer and copy it back. That is not comprehension. Inference, tone, implication, and authorial intent are the skills that actually matter and they are almost never assessed with meaningful feedback.

The Approach

SIGNAL is built around questions that cannot be answered by scanning for keywords. Students read, then reason. When they get something wrong, the feedback does not just give them the correct answer. It walks through the reasoning process so they understand why the wrong choice seemed plausible and what distinguishes it from the right one.

What it includes
  • Passage library spanning fiction, non-fiction, argument, and narrative across reading levels
  • Inference, tone, implication, and authorial purpose question types
  • Explanatory feedback for every answer choice, not just the correct one
  • Annotation tool allowing students to mark and comment before answering
  • Progress tracking by question type so teachers can see where inference breaks down
  • Gradebook sync for scored sessions
Who it is for

Middle and high school English Language Arts teachers who want a tool that teaches comprehension rather than testing recall, with feedback that actually explains the thinking.
